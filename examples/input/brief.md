# Brief: Morning in the tide pools

A 20–30 second nature short about tide pools at dawn on a rocky Pacific
coast. Mood: quiet, luminous, close-up. Follow one hermit crab as the sun
rises: wide establishing shot of the coast, macro shots of anemones and the
crab, ending on the sun clearing the horizon over the water.

Style: photorealistic, golden-hour light, shallow depth of field.
No people, no text overlays.
